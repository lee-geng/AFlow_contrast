Introduce a `custom` preprocessing step to explicitly extract numerical or structured information from the input before passing it to `AnswerGenerate`. This addresses the mismatch between predicted and expected answers by clarifying ambiguous or incomplete input, especially in numeric contexts. This change is based on the failure evidence where the model misinterpreted or miscalculated numerical values (e.g., 10.7 vs 25.6%, or 1.00 vs 1|1.0).
