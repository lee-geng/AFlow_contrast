Added a new prompt `ANSWER_SELECTION_PROMPT` to the `prompt_custom` and modified the `__call__` method in `graph.py` to use the `Custom` operator with this new prompt for final answer selection. This change aims to improve the alignment of the extracted answer with the expected ground truth by explicitly instructing the model to choose the most accurate answer from a list of candidates. This is a small, targeted patch to address the `answer_mismatch` failures.
